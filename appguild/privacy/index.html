AppGuild for App Store Connect

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

AppGuild does not collect any data. There is no AppGuild account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no server run by the developer. You connect with your own App Store Connect API key, which stays in the Keychain on your device, and the app talks directly to Apple.

What we collect

Nothing. AppGuild has no sign-in of its own, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The developer does not receive any information about you or how you use the app. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected.”

Your API key

To connect a live account, you enter your own App Store Connect API key: the Issuer ID, Key ID, and the .p8 private key file, and optionally a vendor number for proceeds. These credentials are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are not written elsewhere on disk, not logged, and not sent to the developer.

The app uses the key on the device to sign short-lived JSON Web Tokens (ES256). Those tokens are what authenticate requests to Apple. The private key itself is not uploaded.

When you add a key, “Sync with iCloud Keychain” is off by default, so the key stays on that device. If you turn the option on, Apple can sync the key to your other devices through iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end. That sync is handled by Apple, not by AppGuild, and you can leave it off.

What the app talks to

AppGuild sends API requests directly from your device to Apple’s App Store Connect API at api.appstoreconnect.apple.com. There is no AppGuild backend in between.

The app may also load your apps’ icons from image URLs Apple includes in that API response. If you tap a link, for example to the Resolution Center or App Store Connect in a browser, that page opens in the usual way on your device. Exchange rates used to show proceeds in your preferred currency are bundled with the app and are not fetched from the network.

If you track keywords in the Keywords tab, the app checks your App Store search position by sending the keyword, the country, and the store (iPhone, iPad, or Mac) from your device to Apple’s public iTunes Search API at itunes.apple.com. These requests use no account, API key, or identifier, and nothing about them is sent to the developer. Position history stays on your device.

Share cards are drawn on your device. An image leaves your device only if you share or save it yourself.

You can use every screen without a key by choosing “Explore with sample data.” Sample mode does not contact Apple or the developer.

Data stored on your device

To make the app work, AppGuild saves a few things locally on your iPhone or iPad:

  • your API credentials, in the Keychain, as described above
  • your preferences, such as display currency, haptics, notification settings, and onboarding state, in on-device storage
  • cached sales reports, so the app does not have to download them again every time
  • a review timeline of version-state changes, recorded on the device from the time you start using the app
  • icon addresses for your apps, so tiles can show the right artwork on the next launch
  • the keywords you track and their search-position history

This information stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Widgets

The Proceeds and Review Status widgets read a small snapshot that the app writes to a shared App Group container on the same device. Widgets do not hold your API key and do not call Apple themselves. That snapshot never leaves the device.

Disconnecting

In Settings, “Disconnect and delete key” removes your key from this device’s Keychain, along with cached reports, the review timeline, widget data, stored icon addresses, and tracked keywords with their history. The key itself stays active in App Store Connect until you revoke it there. If a device is lost, revoke the key in App Store Connect and it stops working everywhere.

Information Apple provides

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics through App Store Connect. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by AppGuild.

Sales figures, reviews, and the other App Store Connect data you see in the app come from your own Apple Developer account, through Apple’s API, and are shown to you on this device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Your continued use of AppGuild after a change means you accept the updated policy.
