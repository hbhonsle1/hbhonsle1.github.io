AppGuild

Your App Store Connect, in full color

A native dashboard for appreneurs and indie developers. See proceeds, App Review status, customer reviews, and TestFlight for every app you ship. Your API key never leaves your device, unless you choose to sync it with iCloud Keychain.

  • iPhone & iPad
  • iOS 17+
  • No account
  • No AppGuild servers
  • Key stays on device

Home base for the apps you ship

  • Today

    Proceeds over 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or 12 months, including in-app purchases and subscriptions, compared with the previous period.

  • Apps & App Review

    Every app’s current version and review state, the attached build, rejection cards, and a timeline of how long each version spent in each state.

  • Reviews

    Customer reviews across all your apps, filtered by rating, country, or “needs reply.” Reply, edit, or delete responses from your phone.

  • TestFlight

    Builds with their beta review state, plus internal and external groups, public links, and testers.

  • Widgets

    Proceeds and Review Status widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, powered by a snapshot cached on your device.

  • Private by design

    You connect with your own App Store Connect API key. There is no AppGuild account and no AppGuild server. The app collects no data. You can also explore every screen with sample data first.
