AstroDodge

Weave through the grid. Don't get caught.

Steer a little astronaut who keeps moving around a wrap-around neon grid until a rock stops it. Swipe to turn, grab crystals to build a combo, and dodge the aliens and laser beams that warp in. One hit ends the run.

  • No ads

  • iPhone
  • iOS 17+
  • 16 languages

A neon grid, one more turn

  • Swipe to steer

    Your astronaut keeps moving until a rock stops it. Swipe to change direction. The edges wrap around to the other side.

  • Endless

    The high-score mode, on a 9×15 board. Grab crystals to build a combo, and dodge the aliens and laser beams that warp in. One hit ends the run.

  • 100 levels

    Four tiers of 25 hand-tuned levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme. The first level of each tier is open; clearing a level opens the next, for 1, 2 or 3 stars.

  • Locker

    Spend the star crystals you earn on 3D astronaut suits and board worlds. The Lunar world is free; the others are bought with crystals.

  • Game Center

    An optional leaderboard for your best Endless score, plus 42 achievements (1,000 points). The game works fine without it.

  • Private by design

    No ads, accounts, analytics or tracking. Progress stays on your iPhone, and can sync through your own iCloud. We never see it.
