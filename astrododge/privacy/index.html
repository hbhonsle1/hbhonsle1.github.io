AstroDodge: Space Grid

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

AstroDodge doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us. Your best score, star crystals, suits and level progress are saved on your iPhone. If you use iCloud, they're also backed up to your own iCloud account so they carry over to your other devices; we never see them. Settings stay on that iPhone. If you sign in to Game Center, Apple's Game Center service receives your scores and achievements so it can show leaderboards. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; AstroDodge doesn't see or store your Game Center details.

By using or continuing to use AstroDodge, you accept this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Use.

What we collect

Nothing. AstroDodge has no sign-in of its own, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The developer does not receive any information about you or how you use the app. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected.”

Data stored on your device

To make the app work, AstroDodge saves a few things locally on your iPhone:

  • your best Endless score, run stats and level progress, including stars
  • the star crystals you've earned and spent, and the suits and worlds you own or have selected
  • achievements you've unlocked in the app
  • your settings, such as sound, music, haptics, Reduce Motion, and whether you've seen the tutorial

This information is not sent to the developer.

iCloud backup

If you're signed in to iCloud on your iPhone, your best score, star crystals, suits, worlds, level progress and in-app achievements are also stored in your own iCloud key-value storage, so they can appear on your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. That storage is provided by Apple, and we never see it. You can turn iCloud off in the Settings app.

Settings such as sound, music, haptics, Reduce Motion, and whether you've seen the tutorial stay on that iPhone and are not synced.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. The game works without it. If you choose to sign in, Apple's Game Center service receives your Endless scores and achievement progress so it can show the leaderboard and achievements. Sign-in, storage and display of that information are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. AstroDodge does not see or store your Game Center account details, and it only reports scores and progress that have increased.

Resetting progress

In Settings, “Reset progress” (tap twice to confirm) clears your best score, star crystals, suits, worlds, stats and in-app achievements on that iPhone and in iCloud. Game Center scores and achievements stay with Apple. This cannot be undone.

Information Apple provides

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics through App Store Connect. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by AstroDodge.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Your continued use of AstroDodge after a change means you accept the updated policy.
