AstroDodge: Space Grid

Support

Answers to common questions. Can’t find yours? Email us, we read everything.

Playing

How do I play?

Swipe to change direction. Your astronaut keeps moving around a wrap-around grid until a rock stops it. Grab crystals quickly to build a combo, and dodge the aliens and laser beams that warp in. One hit ends the run. The edges wrap around to the other side. A short tutorial appears the first time you play; you can show it again from Settings.

What’s the difference between Endless and Levels?

Endless is the high-score mode, on a 9×15 board. Survive as long as you can and beat your best score. Levels is 100 hand-tuned puzzles with goals such as collecting a number of crystals, sometimes against a clock, on boards from 7×11 to 11×17.

How do levels and tiers work?

There are four tiers of 25 levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme. The first level of each tier is open; clearing a level opens the next one in that tier. Each level can be cleared with 1, 2 or 3 stars, depending on how you finish it.

How do star crystals and the Locker work?

Star crystals you earn in play can be spent in the Locker on 3D astronaut suits and board worlds. The Lunar world is free; the others are bought with crystals.

Which devices and languages does AstroDodge support?

AstroDodge is an iPhone app for iOS 17 or later, and plays in portrait and landscape. It is translated into 16 languages. The game itself works offline; iCloud sync and Game Center need a network connection.

Your progress

How does iCloud sync work?

If you're signed in to iCloud on your iPhone, your best score, star crystals, suits, worlds, level progress and in-app achievements are backed up to your own iCloud account so they can appear on your other devices. We never see this data. Sound, music, haptics and whether you've seen the tutorial stay on that iPhone and are not synced.

How do I restore my progress on a new device?

On the new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple ID (and the same iCloud account), then install AstroDodge. Your progress should appear after iCloud finishes syncing. Sign in to Game Center separately if you want your leaderboard score and Game Center achievements.

How do I reset my progress?

In Settings, tap Reset progress, then tap again to confirm. This clears your best score, star crystals, suits, worlds, stats and in-app achievements on that iPhone and in iCloud. Game Center scores and achievements stay with Apple. This cannot be undone.

Game Center

Do I need Game Center?

No. Game Center is optional, and AstroDodge works fine without it. Sign in if you want the leaderboard and achievements. Signing in is handled by Apple, in the Settings app or when the Game Center sheet appears.

How do the leaderboard and achievements work?

There is one leaderboard for your best Endless score, and 42 achievements totalling 1,000 points. Progress is reported to Apple only when it increases. If you sign in later, the app catches up your saved best score and any achievements you've already earned.
