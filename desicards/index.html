Everything you need for game night

  • Would You Rather + Ask Me

    Two card types: impossible desi choices, and questions that get everyone talking.

  • Family, couples or friends

    Pick who's playing and get cards written for that group.

  • Mild or Masala

    Keep it sweet, or turn up the spice. Family games are always Mild, and the two never mix.

  • 8 topic decks

    Food, desi parents, weddings, festivals, Bollywood, cricket, travel in India and NRI life.

  • Favourites & your own decks

    Heart any card, then build named decks for road trips, Diwali night and more.

  • Share as an image

    Send any card to the group chat as a festive card picture.

  • Suggest a card

    Got a great question? Write it in the app and send it to us from your own mail app.

  • New cards first

    Optionally deal cards you haven't played yet before ones you've seen.

  • Works offline

    Every card is built in, so it plays on flights, road trips and at nani's house.
